The meaning of طيف

What remains after the moment has passed.

In Arabic, tayf is a trace — a presence that passes and leaves an impression. TAYF was created around that idea: fragrance is remembered long after the room has emptied.

We curate Arabian perfumery — oud, amber, saffron, bakhoor-warm resins — alongside the fresh, modern scents that live comfortably beside them. Photographed honestly, described plainly, ordered simply.

About TAYF

The lingering presence of fragrance.

طيف — tayf — is the Arabic word for a trace: a presence that passes through a room and stays behind in the air. It is the closest word we know for what a good perfume does.

Scent and memory are bound together. A single note can return you to a place, a season, a person. In Arab culture, fragrance has always been part of how we welcome, celebrate and remember — oud burned for guests, bakhoor drifting through a home, perfume worn as a quiet form of respect.

TAYF curates that tradition for today. We select fragrances from established Arabian houses — the deep ambers and ouds alongside the fresh, contemporary scents that share their spirit — photograph them honestly, and describe them plainly so you can discover what suits you.

Scents that linger. Memories that remain.
